SUNSET BAY is an original top-down open-world crime game that runs entirely in the browser. Explore a sun-soaked city of downtown towers, a neon nightlife quarter, palm-lined beaches, a working harbour and quiet suburbs. Steal and drive cars, take on a five-mission story from fixer Rina, earn side cash as a taxi or courier, collect golden stars, and survive police chases that escalate from one to five wanted stars. Every sound and the soundtrack are synthesised in code.

## Run it
```sh
node serve.ts
```

Open the URL it prints in desktop Chrome. Move with WASD, aim and fire with the mouse, sprint with Shift, enter or exit vehicles with E, switch weapons with Q, reload with R, toggle rain with T, save with F6 and pause with P.

Try the deterministic screenshot scenes at `?shot=cover`, `?shot=foot`, `?shot=drive` and `?shot=chase`.
